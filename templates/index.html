How it works

The way you actually work.

NotionLess isn't another note app. It's a thinking workspace built for people who maintain guides, document systems, and share quickly. Every interaction is optimized for speed — from the ghost hint on new lines to the shortcut HUD that appears the moment you need it.

Building knowledge

Hierarchical pages. Nested guides. Zero clutter.

Every new user gets a Welcome page pre-loaded with what NotionLess supports and how to use it. From there, pages nest naturally: a top-level guide, sub-pages for chapters, deeper levels for references. Drag, drop, expand, collapse. Your structure stays readable no matter how large it grows.

  • / Open the block command menu anywhere
  • Tab / Shift+Tab Nest or unnest blocks
  • Alt+Insert Create a subpage instantly
  • Alt++ / - Expand or collapse the entire tree

Speed first

Keyboard before mouse. HUD before guesswork.

Hold Alt or Ctrl and the shortcut HUD appears with everything available. No menus to memorize. Auto-save runs at 2 seconds, so nothing is lost. The sidebar label updates in real time — Ready, Auto-saving..., Saved at 14:32. Click it and you get the full notification history, timestamped, scrollable, capped at 100.

  • Ctrl+S Manual save when you want certainty
  • Ctrl+K Quick-switcher overlay
  • Alt+PgUp / PgDn Browse pages in order
  • Ctrl+Z / Shift+Z Undo and redo

Share quickly

Public pages. Public walls. No account required for readers.

Toggle a page public with 🔒 / 🌐. When public, all subpages become public too — a cascade that keeps guides coherent. Every user has a public wall at /wall/<username> showing everything they've shared. Visitors can view and copy public pages to their own workspace. The original authors stay tracked in the page metadata.

  • 🌐 Toggle visibility from the workspace
  • Copy Any public page to your profile
  • /search Find users and their public guides
