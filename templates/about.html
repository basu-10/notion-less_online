Philosophy

NotionLess started with a simple observation: most note-taking apps ask you to trade convenience for privacy. The cloud promises sync and accessibility, but it also means your thoughts are stored on someone else's servers.

This project explores a different path. What if we built a fully-functional editor that simply respects your data? Each account has its own private database, and your notes are stored securely on the server — always accessible, always yours.

The cloud-first approach isn't a limitation — it's a practical choice. Your notes should be yours, stored where you can access them from anywhere. Each account gets its own isolated SQLite database for reliable storage.
