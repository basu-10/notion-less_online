Notion, but less.

Its a Notion like editor, without the AI and cloud smarts.

This one lives in YOUR device (browser).

No accounts, no sync, no noise.

Block-based editing

Rich block types via slash commands. Nest, reorder, and style content with keyboard-first navigation.

Hierarchical pages

Organize with a collapsible tree. Drag pages into folders, expand sections, keep context.

No one sees your data

Everything saves to IndexedDB in the browser. Your data stays on your device, accessible offline.
